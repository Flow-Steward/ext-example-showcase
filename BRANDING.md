The Example Showcase demonstrates Flow Steward extension contracts and uses the Flow Steward owner's extension mark.

- Official artwork is the [Flow Steward extension icon](https://github.com/Flow-Steward/ext-email-imap/blob/606c75862e0a97252e9985d8e07b8d87cac7e821/icon.png).
- The source and packaged PNG both have SHA-256 `e81950a8b57d2ae07b8210045363fd082f537614c09fb8c7df0252f55d2d4bf5`.
- The PNG is copied byte for byte with its original dimensions, colors, and transparency.
- Flow Steward supplies the mark through its own repository. The image carries no third-party provider artwork.
